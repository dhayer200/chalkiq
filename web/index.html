How it works

01 — Elo ratings

Margin-of-victory scaling

Each team carries a single rating updated after every game. The standard Elo update is multiplied by a margin factor (ln(|m|+1)) with an autocorrelation correction that prevents blowouts against weak teams from inflating ratings. NCAAB: K=24, scale=300, home advantage=100 pts.

02 — Backtesting

Walk-forward, no look-ahead

Predictions use only data available before tip-off. Ratings update in chronological order; no future game outcomes inform any prediction. The Elo scale factor was calibrated on 2024–25 data and frozen before the 2025–26 season began.

03 — Validation

Closing line value

CLV = model probability minus closing market probability (vig-removed). The closing line aggregates sharp bettors and late information. Mean CLV of −1.24% is consistent with market efficiency; the 19.4% std dev reveals where signal lives.
