Methodology & Validation

How we test. What the numbers mean. What they don't.

Walk-Forward Testing

Every prediction is made using only data available before tip-off. The model processes games in chronological order. Elo ratings update after each result. No future information leaks into any prediction.

This is the gold standard for backtesting sports models. Many published systems optimize on the full dataset (look-ahead bias), which inflates accuracy. Ours doesn't.

No Look-Ahead Bias

The Elo scale factor (300) was calibrated on Season 1 data and frozen before Season 2 began. It was not re-tuned to improve Season 2 numbers. What you see is out-of-sample performance.

Season 2 (2025–26) is performing better than the calibration set (+4.71% vs +3.10% CLV), which is the opposite of what you'd see from overfitting.

What CLV Means

Closing Line Value measures whether our predicted edge beats the market's final price. If we say a team has a 65% win probability and the closing line implies 61%, that's +4% CLV.

The closing line is the sharpest number in sports betting—it incorporates all available information right before tip-off. Consistently beating it is the single best predictor of long-term profitability.

What CLV Does Not Mean

Positive CLV does not guarantee profit on any single game, week, or month. Variance is real. November is historically negative for us because Elo needs ~20 games per team to stabilize.

CLV is a process metric, not a results metric. It tells you whether you're making good bets, not whether you won today. Over hundreds of games, process wins.

How ChalkIQ Works

1

Calibrated Elo Engine

Custom scale factor (300 vs standard 400) calibrated against 1,300+ games with real odds data. Minimizes log loss (0.5161) and maximizes CLV. Margin-of-victory scaling, tempo normalization, season-reset regression.

2

Injury Detection

DNP-based injury detection from 1,981 boxscores. When a key player is absent, the model adjusts win probabilities. Injury-adjusted games show +4.60% CLV across 80 games—the market underprices missing players.

3

Market Comparison

Every prediction is compared against opening and closing lines from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and Caesars. We only recommend games where the model finds real edge vs the market.
